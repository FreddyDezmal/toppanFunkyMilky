# Milky Shaky Custom Drinks – Patron guide

How to order milkshakes online and collect them at a Milky Shaky restaurant.

> **Demonstration site.** Payments run in Paystack **test mode**: no real money is taken, and you pay with a test card
> that the facilitator gives you. Emails go only to addresses approved for the demonstration.

## 1. Create an account

1. Open the site and choose **Register**.
2. Enter your first name, email address and mobile number (e.g. `+27 82 123 4567`), choose a password and type it
   again.
3. Choose **Register**. You are signed in straight away.

Your details are under **My account**. To sign in later, choose **Log in** and enter your email and password. After
several wrong passwords in a row your account is locked for a short time; wait and try again.

## 2. Order milkshakes

1. Choose **Order** and enter how many milkshakes you want (1 to 10), then continue.
2. Each milkshake has its own section. For each one choose a **Flavour**, a **Consistency** (how thick) and a
   **Topping**, then choose **Done**. **Done** works only when all three are chosen; you can **Edit** a milkshake
   afterwards.
3. Each milkshake shows its price. The estimate below shows the **Subtotal**, your **Frequent-customer discount**
   (R 0,00 if none applies), **VAT** and the **Total**. You cannot change these amounts; they come from the
   restaurant's current prices.
4. Choose **Continue to pickup**.

## 3. Choose where and when to collect

1. Choose the **Pickup restaurant**.
2. Choose the **Pickup date and time**: at least 15 minutes from now and at most 7 days ahead (South African time).
3. Continue to the review page, check everything and choose **Place order**, then confirm the total.

Your order now has a reference such as `MS-ABCD-1234`. It is **not confirmed until you pay**.

## 4. Pay

1. Choose **Pay now**. You are taken to Paystack's secure payment page; your card details are entered there, never on
   the Milky Shaky site.
2. Pay. You return to the site, which checks the payment with Paystack; after a few seconds your order shows as
   **Paid**.

If you leave the payment page without paying, nothing is charged: open the order from **My orders** and choose
**Pay now** again.

## 5. Your receipt

When the payment is confirmed you receive one email, "Order MS-… confirmed – your receipt". It is your order
confirmation and your receipt: the milkshakes, restaurant and pickup time, VAT, the total paid, the payment reference
and a link to your order. Check your spam folder if it has not arrived within a few minutes.

## 6. Your orders

**My orders** lists all your orders, newest first. Open one to see its details, payment status, and any
cancellation or refund. Only you can see your orders.

## 7. Cancelling an order

- **You cancel:** open a paid order before its pickup time and choose **Cancel order**. The page shows the
  cancellation fee (10 % of the amount paid) and the refund before you confirm. The refund is sent back through
  Paystack; the order shows the refund as pending until Paystack confirms it, and you receive an email.
- **An unpaid order** can be cancelled at no cost.
- **The restaurant asks you to cancel:** you receive an email and see the request on the order. Choose **Accept cancellation**
  for a full refund with no fee, or decline it to keep your order. If you do not answer before the pickup time, your order
  stays as it is.
- If a refund cannot be completed automatically, you are emailed and asked to contact the restaurant.

## 8. Help

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Done" does nothing | Choose a flavour, consistency and topping for that milkshake |
| A pickup time is refused | Choose a time at least 15 minutes from now and within the next 7 days |
| The order still shows unpaid after paying | Wait a few seconds and refresh the order; if it stays unpaid, contact the restaurant with the order reference – do not pay twice |
| No email | Check spam; the order page always has the same details |
| Locked out after wrong passwords | Wait a few minutes and try again |
